## 8. Authorization boundary

Technical approval does not authorize merge, publication, deployment, irreversible actions or scope expansion. The responsible human retains those decisions. Completing this record does not grant permission to post it remotely or submit a formal provider approval.

A reviewer who implemented the assessed change cannot approve it as an independent reviewer. This template contains no actual review, vendor requirement, completed evidence or project authorization.
